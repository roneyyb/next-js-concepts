Cookies are small pieces of data that server sends to users web browser

The browser may store the cookie and send it back to the same server with later request.

Cookies are mainly user for three purposes

Session management like logins and shopping carts
Personalization like user preferences and themes
Traking like recording and analyzing user behaviour

Cookies are small text files that websites send to your browser to identify you and remember your preferences.
 They can make your browsing experience easier and more personalized. 
How cookies work
When you visit a website, your browser sends data to the website's server. 
The server sends back a cookie to your browser. 
The cookie is stored in a temporary folder on your device. 
The cookie helps the website remember your preferences and browsing history. 
How cookies are used
Shopping carts: E-commerce websites use cookies to remember what's in your cart between visits. 
Targeted ads: Advertisers use cookies to track your browsing habits and show you ads based on your interests. 
Logins: Websites use cookies to remember your login information. 
Cookie privacy
Third-party cookies can be a privacy concern because they allow companies to track your behavior without your knowledge. Unsecured cookies can also be a security risk.
